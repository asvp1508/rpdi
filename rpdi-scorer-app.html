RPDI Scorer

Enforces the current claim distribution rule: Spine 10% (capped at 3, rounded down), Soul 15%, Skeleton/Flesh/Skin 25% each — Skin absorbs rounding. Minimum 20 claims, no maximum. You judge every claim's accuracy — this tool only does the math and flags what needs a second look.

The Formula

        
D = 1 − (Σ weight × accuracy) / (Σ weight)
L = (wS·S + wP·P + wF·F + wE·E) / (wS+wP+wF+wE)
RPDI = 0.6(D) + 0.4(L)
Gap = |D − L|

Spine-Floor Rule

        

          If any Spine claim scores ≤0.2, OR the single tagged Absolute Premise claim scores ≤0.3,
          the final classification cannot register below High — regardless of the raw weighted average.
          Raw D is still calculated and shown; only the label overrides. A fabricated central premise
          disqualifies a film from a low score, no matter how accurate the background details are.

Feel Score Weighting Rule

        

          All four dimensions (S/P/F/E) default to weight 1. A weight may only be raised with specific,
          citable evidence from the film itself — never genre assumption alone.
        

      


      

        
Known Limitations

        

          Single-rater subjectivity (no inter-rater check exists). Ceilings and the 0.6/0.4 RPDI
          weighting are judgment calls, not derived constants. Gap thresholds are provisional.
          Skin's actual share drifts slightly above 25% at higher claim counts since it absorbs
          rounding. No film has yet been fully re-verified under this current rule set.
